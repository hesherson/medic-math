Lazarus Privacy Policy

Lazarus is a Sanguine Solutions product. Effective October 20, 2026.

The short version

Lazarus does not collect, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What is stored, and where

  • Your progress, lesson results, review queue, settings, study set, protocol pack, class assignments and code timer logs are saved in the app's local storage on your device.
  • Nothing is sent to Sanguine Solutions or any other server. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes all of it.
  • The backup feature in Settings creates a file only when you ask for it, and you choose where it goes.

Sharing you start yourself

When you share a protocol pack, an assignment code, a result line, a code timer log or a PDF, Lazarus hands it to the share sheet or saves it where you choose. What happens next depends on the app you send it to. Lazarus does not keep a copy anywhere else.

Permissions

  • Notifications (Android): only if you turn on the daily reminder. The reminder is scheduled on your device.
  • Internet: the app works offline. The web version loads its own files from GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, can see standard request information such as your IP address.

Children

Lazarus is a study tool for EMS students and providers. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Medical content

Lazarus is for education. Doses and protocols vary by agency. Always follow your local protocols and medical direction.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact email on the Lazarus Google Play listing.
