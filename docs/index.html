Welcome

One rule, every drug

Medic Math is built around a single idea, and everything else grows out of it: percent means grams in every hundred millilitres. Once that sits comfortably, every dilution, every drip and every drug calculation turns into the same handful of moves, which you'll find laid out step by step under Learn, drilled into reflex under Drill, and applied to real drugs in the library, where the dose calculator runs live against whatever patient weight you set.

Clock

How fast do you want to be?

Timed questions are what turn knowing into reflex, so pick a clock you can live with for now and tighten it as you improve. Thirty seconds is a fair place to start.

Patient

Default weight

Every drug card doses against one patient weight, and you can change it any time from the chip in the top corner, so set a sensible default here.

Daily

Ten minutes a day

Ten minutes a day is the whole plan. Today lines up four short things for you, flash cards that resurface right before you'd forget them, a quick quiz aimed at whatever you've been missing, a drug check and one scenario step, and the streak counts the days you show up rather than the questions you answer.
